Scanabowl was made by a cat owner from the Netherlands. I wanted to know what is really in my own cat's food, and found out how hard that is. The front of the pack promises everything: “natural”, “premium”, “rich in chicken”. What is actually inside is in small print on the back, which is hard to read and even harder to compare.

I also noticed how often advice about pet food comes from the pet food industry itself: from brands, from sellers who earn more on one brand, or from research paid for by manufacturers. That is not necessarily wrong, but I wanted a place where you can see the numbers for yourself, without a brand deciding what you read.

That is how Scanabowl started. Every food is rated with the same formula, based on public guidelines (FEDIAF) and the research we list on the [method page](/en/how-we-score). No brand can buy a better score or a place in a list, and every score explains why it came out the way it did.

Scanabowl does not give veterinary advice. The score describes the label, not your pet. If your pet has a medical condition, always talk to your vet.
