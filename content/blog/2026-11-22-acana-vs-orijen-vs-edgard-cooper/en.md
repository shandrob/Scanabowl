If you are shopping for pet food with lots of fresh meat and recognizable ingredients, **Acana**, **Orijen**, and **Edgard & Cooper** are almost guaranteed to land on your shortlist. All three position themselves as premium alternatives to supermarket and vet-clinic staples — yet their labels reveal clear differences.

## Acana vs. Orijen: same maker, different protein level

Many pet owners do not realize that **Acana and Orijen are made by the same producer** (Champion Petfoods, now owned by Mars). Both use high shares of named animal ingredients (meat, fish, and organs) and avoid vague group terms.

The main difference is **intensity**:
- **[Orijen](/en/foods/brand/orijen)** pushes furthest: averaging **95/100** for dogs and **93/100** for cats, with very high protein levels and dense animal-ingredient lists.
- **[Acana](/en/foods/brand/acana)** is slightly more moderate (and usually a bit easier on the wallet): averaging **90/100** for dogs and **87/100** for cats. For dogs that get soft stools on ultra-high-protein formulas, Acana is often an easier fit.

## Edgard & Cooper: standout wet food, more modest kibble

**[Edgard & Cooper](/en/foods/brand/edgard-en-cooper)** shows a striking split between its wet and dry lines:

- **Wet food scores very well:** averaging **86/100** for dogs (up to 95) and **81/100** for cats, with generous named meat and clean formulations.
- **Dry kibble scores lower:** averaging **67/100** for dogs and **65/100** for cats. Because their kibble leads with fresh meat (which is ~70% water before cooking), potatoes, peas, and plant ingredients end up making up a larger share of the dry matter than the front of the bag suggests.

Learn more about fresh vs. dehydrated meat on labels in [How to read a pet food label](/en/blog/2026-09-21-etiket-lezen).

## Which brand fits your pet?

- **Looking for top-scoring kibble?** **[Orijen](/en/foods/brand/orijen)** (95) and **[Acana](/en/foods/brand/acana)** (90) sit near the very top of our entire catalog.
- **Feeding wet food or mixing wet and dry?** **[Edgard & Cooper](/en/foods/brand/edgard-en-cooper)** wet food (86 for dogs, 81 for cats) is an excellent, widely available option.
- **Take transitions slowly:** Moving from a standard grain kibble to a protein-dense recipe like Orijen or Acana works best over 7–10 days. See [How to switch pet food safely](/en/blog/2026-10-01-overstappen-ander-voer).
